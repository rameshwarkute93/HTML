MS Dhoni

Nickname : Captain Cool, Thala

Born : 7 July 1981

Birthplace : Ranchi, Jharkhand, India

Nationality : Indian

MS Dhoni is one of India's greatest cricketers and captains and is popularly known as the "Captain Cool." Born on 7 July 1981 in Ranchi, Jharkhand, he is a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter famous for his calm personality, powerful finishing, and exceptional leadership. He made his international debut in 2004 and became one of the most successful captains in Indian cricket. Dhoni led India to the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy titles. He is also famous for his iconic helicopter shot and his ability to finish matches under pressure. In the IPL, he has been one of the most successful captains with Chennai Super Kings. His combination of calmness, leadership, wicketkeeping skills, finishing ability, and cricketing intelligence has made him a legendary figure in Indian cricket.
