KL Rahul

Nickname : KL, Stylish Batter

Born : 18 April 1992

Birthplace : Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

Nationality : Indian

KL Rahul is one of India's talented modern cricketers and is known for his stylish and technically sound batting. Born on 18 April 1992 in Bengaluru, Karnataka, he is a right-handed batter who can also contribute as a wicketkeeper. He made his international debut in 2014 and has represented India across all three formats. Rahul is famous for his elegant stroke play, versatility, and ability to bat in different positions according to the team's requirements. He has scored centuries in Test, ODI, and T20I cricket and has also been an important player in the IPL. His combination of technique, timing, versatility, wicketkeeping skills, and adaptability has made him a valuable player for Indian cricket.
