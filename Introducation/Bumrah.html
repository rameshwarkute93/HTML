Jasprit Bumrah

Nickname : Yorker King, Boom Boom

Born : 6 December 1993

Birthplace : Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

Nationality : Indian

Jasprit Bumrah is one of India's greatest fast bowlers and is popularly known as the "Yorker King." Born on 6 December 1993 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, he is a right-arm fast bowler famous for his unique bowling action, accurate yorkers, pace, and ability to take wickets at crucial moments. He made his international debut in 2016 and quickly became one of the most important members of the Indian cricket team. Bumrah is especially dangerous in the death overs because of his accurate yorkers and variations. He has performed exceptionally well across all three formats and has established himself as one of the best fast bowlers in modern cricket. His combination of pace, accuracy, yorkers, variations, and consistency has made him one of the most feared bowlers in world cricket.
