Virat Kohli

Nickname : King Kohli, Chase Master

Born : 5 November 1988

Birthplace : Delhi, India

Nationality : Indian

Virat Kohli is one of India's greatest modern cricketers and is popularly known as the "King Kohli." Born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, he is a right-handed top-order batter famous for his excellent technique, aggressive batting, fitness, and ability to chase difficult targets. He made his international debut in 2008 and has represented India in all three formats. Virat is one of the greatest run-scorers and century makers in international cricket and is especially famous for his outstanding performances in ODI cricket. As captain, he led India to many memorable victories and helped build a strong fitness culture in Indian cricket. His combination of consistency, determination, aggression, fitness, and leadership has made him one of the most respected cricketers in the world.
