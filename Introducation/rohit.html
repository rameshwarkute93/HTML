Rohit Sharma

Nickname : Hitman, Ro

Born : 30 April 1987

Birthplace : Nagpur, Maharashtra, India

Nationality : Indian

Rohit Sharma is one of India’s greatest modern cricketers and is popularly known as the “Hitman.” Born on 30 April 1987 in Nagpur, Maharashtra, he is a right-handed opening batter famous for his excellent timing, powerful pull shots, and ability to score big runs. He made his international debut in 2007 and has represented India in all three formats. Rohit is the only batter to score three ODI double-centuries, with his highest ODI score being a world-record 264 runs against Sri Lanka. He also scored five centuries in the 2019 ODI World Cup, the most by any batter in a single World Cup edition. As captain, he led India to the 2024 T20 World Cup and 2025 Champions Trophy titles. In the IPL, he has won six titles, including five as captain of Mumbai Indians. Rohit is also one of the most successful six-hitters in international cricket and has scored over 11,000 ODI runs. His combination of batting ability, leadership, calm personality, and record-breaking performances has made him one of the most respected cricketers in the world.
